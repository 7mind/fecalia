## Remaining production defect

Whole-bind `Close` can miss a late lazily published FEC owner:

- `ensurePeerReceiveInstantiated` constructs/starts and stores the owner under
  only the peer lifecycle mutex at `internal/bind/multipath.go:2059-2086`.
- Whole-bind Close performs one sender detachment at `:3767-3780`, retires that
  captured set, and then waits for readers at `:3782-3785`.
- A receive loop entering through `:2440-2445` can publish after the one-time
  detachment.
- `clearPerOpenStateAfterReaders` at `:3819-3835` does not swap, stop, or wait
  `fecSend`.
- A subsequent Open overwrites the pointer at `:1958-1964`, leaving the old
  owner orphaned and permitting generation overlap.

This differs from `TearDownPeer`, which now retains the same peer's
`lifecycleMu` through swap, Stop, and Wait.

Required deterministic witness: block target lazy owner publication, start
Close, prove the initial detachment passed with a sentinel owner, release the
target publication, let Close return, then assert `fecSend` is nil and the
captured owner exited. The fix must prevent publication after closing begins or
perform a final post-reader detach/Stop/Wait, never while holding `m.mu`.
