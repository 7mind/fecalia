# T309 implementation review R4 B

Verdict: **disapprove**

Reviewed commit: `6994ecb95c5ffb1143c46db4b18b3d688fb7b779`

## Stable evidence

- One goroutine mutates each published encoder/controller.
- Exact due checks precede admission.
- Open groups remain writer-invisible.
- Payload staging remains bounded to one group.
- Focused ordinary and race tests pass.
- A writer error terminates the decided group without same-group retry; the owner intentionally remains available for later calls.
- Encoder ownership tests cover success, `M=0`, and coding failure.

## Blocking defects

1. **Per-frame request boundary and up-front sequence reservation**

   `internal/bind/multipath.go:3192-3217` and `internal/bind/fec_sender.go:81-90` allocate one request plus two channels per frame and reserve all outer sequences before the owner admits them. This violates T309's single batch request and can consume sequence numbers for a suffix never admitted after an earlier group fails.

2. **Post-publication stop race**

   At `internal/bind/fec_sender.go:170-181`, a published request can return on stop before its admission acknowledgement. The caller may then reuse its buffer while the owner reads it at `:313-315`.

3. **Adaptive mutex inversion**

   `internal/bind/multipath.go:3459-3476` synchronously waits on owner submission while holding `m.mu`; owner decision/adaptive work at `internal/bind/fec_sender.go:459-473` can wait on that mutex before consuming the control request.

4. **Peer owner overlap during teardown/rebind**

   `internal/bind/multipath.go:2673-2685` releases `lifecycleMu` before joining the old owner. `ensurePeerReceiveInstantiated` at `:2059-2086` can publish a replacement while the old owner still exits.

## Contract question

`internal/bind/fec_sender.go:419-436` sends a decided group through the shaped seam one datagram per call, while `docs/design.md:1360-1363` describes complete-group handoff. The revision must either submit the immutable group as one batch with correct terminal-prefix accounting or align the documented contract and tests without changing legacy writer-error behavior.

## Metrics decision

T309 requires bind-local deadline-miss and overshoot observability and explicitly excludes a new Prometheus series. No metrics-surface expansion belongs in this task.
